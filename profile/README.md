## About the Organization

</div>

**360 Muslim Experts** is a Pakistan-based organization dedicated to empowering the Ummah through knowledge, skills, and service. Headquartered in Lahore, it operates as an international professional network connecting scholars, medical professionals, and technical engineers for constructive collaboration and high-impact initiatives.

The organization combines Islamic learning with modern professional disciplines, fostering a global collective committed to education, community welfare, and innovation.

- **Innovation** — Bridging tradition with modern solutions
